# Mental Arithmetic Pro — Design Direction

## Three Directions

### Theme Name: Paper Sprint
Very warm, editorial learning tool with tactile paper texture, ink-like marks, and energetic orange accents. It feels like a smart notebook turned into a focused practice studio.

**Probability:** 0.07

### Theme Name: Neon Abacus
A dark, arcade-like practice room with bright counters, electric feedback, and game-session intensity. It makes speed and streaks feel competitive.

**Probability:** 0.04

### Theme Name: Quiet Orbit
A calm, spacious study companion with soft blue-gray surfaces, circular progress geometry, and restrained motion. It prioritizes consistency and clarity over spectacle.

**Probability:** 0.08

## Chosen Approach: Paper Sprint

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial learning design with risograph print texture, soft clay illustration, and utilitarian Swiss information hierarchy.

### Core Principles
1. Make every practice decision immediately legible.
2. Pair playful warmth with serious progress feedback.
3. Use asymmetry and paper-like layers to make the interface feel crafted.
4. Turn repetition into a visible ritual through streaks, session cards, and small rewards.

### Color Philosophy
Deep ink navy is the trustworthy workspace; warm tangerine is the signal for action and speed; mint is the breath between exercises; cream is the paper that makes the whole product feel approachable. The palette should feel energetic without becoming childish.

### Layout Paradigm
An asymmetric dashboard: a narrow navigation rail, a wide practice stage, and a right-side progress column. The exercise card anchors attention while secondary stats orbit it rather than competing in a uniform grid.

### Signature Elements
Hand-drawn tick marks and progress arcs; layered cream cards with offset orange shadows; an abacus-bead motif used as status indicators and navigation accents.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like turning a page or placing a bead: quick, tactile, and confirmed. Answer submissions use decisive motion; feedback is immediate; mistakes remain calm and useful rather than punitive.

### Animation
Use 150–220ms ease-out transitions for controls. Let number cards enter with a slight vertical lift and opacity shift. Correct answers briefly scale the score badge to 1.04; wrong answers use a soft horizontal nudge. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System
Use Fraunces for display numbers and editorial headlines; use DM Sans for interface copy, labels, and data. Headlines are compact and expressive; supporting copy stays at 14–16px with generous line height. Numbers should have tabular numerals where possible.

### Brand Essence
A focused, playful mental arithmetic studio for ambitious students who want speed, accuracy, and visible progress — without boring drills.

**Personality:** sharp, warm, motivating.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are direct and confident. CTAs sound like invitations to a challenge, not generic onboarding. Microcopy is short, encouraging, and specific.

Example lines:
- “Bugun miyangni tezlashtir.”
- “Yana 3 ta to‘g‘ri javob — yangi rekord.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The mark is a single orange abacus bead intersected by a navy lightning notch. The wordmark should be set in a custom-feeling Fraunces display treatment, with the bead mark replacing the dot on the “i” when used in future branding.

### Signature Brand Color
**Sprint Tangerine — #F47B4A.** It owns the moment of action: start, answer, continue, improve.

## Style Decisions

- Paper Sprint surfaces include tactile print cues: grain, offset shadows, stamped labels, or hand-drawn marks.
- The abacus bead is the primary motif for navigation state, streaks, progress, records, and rewards.
- Sprint Tangerine `#F47B4A` is reserved for action and achievement moments.
